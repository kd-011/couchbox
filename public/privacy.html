Privacy and terms

Games is a free party game site with no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

What is stored

  • One-phone games stay on your device (in your browser's local storage) so a refresh doesn't lose your game. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Rooms (everyone on their own phone) keep the names you type and the game in progress in the server's memory only. There is no database. A room is deleted 15 minutes after everyone leaves, after 3 hours without activity, or whenever the server restarts.
  • Your browser keeps a random room key so you can rejoin after a refresh. Clearing site data removes it.
  • The server keeps short operational logs (room created or closed, errors) without names or IP addresses.

Drinking variants

Drinking versions are for adults of legal drinking age. Every drink prompt is a suggestion: swap any sip for water or a forfeit. Nobody has to drink, and nobody should drive. Look after each other.
