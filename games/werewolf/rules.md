# Werewolf

A handful of players are secretly werewolves. Everyone else is the village.
Each night the wolves hunt. Each day the village argues and lynches someone.
Repeat until one side is wiped out.

1. **Roles are dealt in secret.** Everyone gets a role: Werewolf, Seer,
   Doctor, or Villager. The number of wolves scales with the table (1 under
   8 players, 2 from 8, 3 from 12) and is always kept a minority.
2. **Night falls.** The werewolves secretly pick who to kill (they can see
   and change each other's pick until everyone's chosen, or the clock runs
   out). If a Seer is in play, they learn one player's true role. If a
   Doctor is in play, they pick one player to protect. They are never told
   whether it mattered.
3. **Dawn breaks.** The village learns who died in the night, and that
   player's role, or that nobody died.
4. **Discuss.** The village argues it out before the timer runs out.
5. **Vote.** Everyone still alive votes to lynch someone. On one phone, vote
   out loud and the host taps who got voted out. A tie means nobody is out.
6. **The lynched player's role is revealed.** If a side has now won, the
   host reveals the full ending. Otherwise, night falls again.
7. **Dead players become ghosts.** You can keep watching everything in the
   open, but you take no more night actions and don't vote.

**Village wins** once every werewolf is gone. **Werewolves win** once the
living wolves are at least as many as everyone else still standing.

**Scores (every tier):** village wins, every survivor scores 1. Werewolves
win, every surviving wolf scores 2.

**Drinking:** village wins, every werewolf (caught or not) drinks 3.
Werewolves win, every surviving villager drinks 2.

**Deadly:** whoever the village lynches takes a shot on the way out, right
as their role is revealed. Each new night may also bring a Deadly card:
Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

**Play again** deals a fresh game with new roles and resets scores for the
new game.

All narration and flavour text is original to this build.
