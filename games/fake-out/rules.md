# Fake Out

Everyone sees an obscure true fact with the key detail missing. Write a
convincing fake answer to fill the blank, then the group votes on which
answer, the fakes or the real one, is actually true.

**Scores (every tier):** find the truth and you score a point. Fool someone
with your fake and you score a point for every voter who picked it. Being
fooled scores the fooler, not the fooled. The game runs for the number of
rounds you set (6 by default).

**Drinking:** every voter fooled by a decoy drinks 1. If nobody falls for
your fake, you drink 1 for the dud. At the end, lowest score drinks 2 and
top score hands out 2. If everyone is tied, the whole table drinks 1.

**Deadly:** fool every other player with your fake (at least two of them)
and instead of sipping, the Shot Roulette spins between the fooled and one
of them takes a shot. At the end, last place takes a shot (a tie for last
spins the wheel). Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette,
Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

**On one phone:** the phone is passed round so everyone can write their
fake, and vote, in private. Nobody sees another player's answer or vote
until the reveal.

**Teams:** the host can split the room into teams in the lobby. Everyone
still writes and votes alone, but at the end the lowest-scoring team drinks
together and the top team hands drinks out together.

**The crowd:** join a full room and you watch from the audience instead.
Vote on which answer you think is true, just for yourself: wrong, and you
drink too, but it never changes anyone else's score or fake.

The facts in this game are original writing for Fake Out: real, obscure
trivia across history, science, nature, geography, language, space, food,
sport and invention, each written fresh for this game rather than drawn
from any trivia set, app or book.
