# Sketchy

One player draws a secret word. Everyone else watches it appear live and races to guess it.

1. **See the word.** The drawer gets a private look at the secret word, then taps "Got it, start drawing".
2. **Draw.** The drawer sketches it on their canvas. On one phone, hold it up where everyone can see and draw directly on it. On your own phones, everyone else watches the drawing appear on their screen in real time.
3. **Guess.** On your own phones, type your guess as soon as you know it. On one phone, shout it out and the host taps whoever got it.
4. **Score and rotate.** Faster correct guesses score more. Everyone takes a turn drawing.

**Scores (every tier):** the first correct guesser scores 3, the second 2, everyone else who gets it scores 1. The drawer scores a point for every guesser who gets it. Most points after the last turn wins.

**Drinking:** nobody guesses it before time runs out, the drawer drinks 2. Go the whole game without ever guessing one right, drink 3 at the end.

**Deadly:** nobody guesses your drawing, you take a shot. Go the whole game without ever guessing one right, take a shot at the end. Between turns a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

**Teams:** the host can split the room into teams in the lobby. Nobody guessing the drawing is a team moment in Deadly: the drawer's whole team shares one shot instead of the drawer alone.

**The crowd:** join a full room and you watch from the audience instead. You still see the drawing live and can guess for fun, no scoring or drinking either way.
