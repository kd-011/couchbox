# Paranoia

Every round, one player (the Asker) privately reads a secret question off their
phone: something like "Who at this table would be most likely to ___?"
Nobody else ever sees it.

1. **Read it.** The Asker reads the question in private, then hides it.
2. **Pick a target.** Out loud, the Asker picks someone at the table and leans
   in close to quietly paraphrase the question to them, away from everyone
   else's ears.
3. **Answer out loud.** The Target says a name out loud, whoever they think
   the answer is. The Asker records who got named.
4. **Flip the coin.** The moment the answer is recorded, a coin decides:
   heads, the question gets read out to the whole table. Tails, it's gone
   forever. Nobody controls it, and nobody gets a say.

**Scores (every tier):** the Asker always scores 1 point for keeping the
round moving, whether or not the coin reveals anything. The game runs for
the number of rounds you set (8 by default). Turn on **Spicy questions** in
set-up for a sharper, more personal pool.

**Drinking:** if the coin reveals the question, the named player sips 2.
Nothing happens if it stays secret.

**Deadly:** if the coin reveals the question, the named player takes a shot.
Between rounds a Deadly card may also turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a
Waterfall or a water round.

**On one phone:** the Asker gets the one private peek at the question. After
that, the phone becomes the shared screen for the table: whoever is holding
it taps out the target and the named answer on everyone's behalf, since
there's nowhere else for those taps to come from.

All questions are original to this game.
