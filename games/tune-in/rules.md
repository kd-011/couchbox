# Tune In

One player, the Psychic, rotates every round. They privately see a hidden target
somewhere on a dial between two opposite ideas (say, "Overrated" and
"Underrated"). They give a one-word or short clue meant to point the rest of
the table at roughly where the target sits. Everyone else sets a guess on the
dial, and the whole table is scored on how close the guess landed.

1. **The Psychic looks.** Only they see the target. Everyone else just waits.
2. **The Psychic gives a clue.** One word or a short phrase. Once it's given,
   the two ends of the spectrum and the clue are shown to everyone; the
   target stays hidden.
3. **The table guesses.** On your own phones, everyone except the Psychic
   drags their own dial and the group's guess is the average. On one shared
   phone, the whole table talks it out and nudges one dial together, then the
   host locks it in.
4. **Reveal.** The target and the final guess appear side by side on the dial.

**Scoring (every tier):** a bullseye (within 3) scores 3 points each for the
Psychic and everyone who guessed, "close" (within 10) scores 2, "okay"
(within 20) scores 1, anything further is a miss and scores nothing. Scoring
is cooperative: the table either reads the clue well together or it doesn't.
The game runs for the number of rounds you set (8 by default).

**Drinking:** a wild miss (the worst band) costs the Psychic 2 sips. A bad
clue is on them.

**Deadly:** a wild miss costs the Psychic a shot instead. Between rounds a
Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall, Social, or a
water round.

All spectrum pairs are original to this site.
