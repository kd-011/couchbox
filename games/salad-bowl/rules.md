# Salad Bowl

Everyone writes names and phrases into a shared pot. Then you play three rounds through the same pot, a different clue rule each round.

1. **Fill the bowl.** Everyone writes a few names or phrases in private. Nobody sees anyone else's until it is pulled from the pot.
2. **Describe.** One player at a time pulls a phrase and describes it to the table, saying anything except the word itself. Get as many as you can before the buzzer.
3. **One Word.** Same pot, reshuffled. This time your clue is a single word, nothing more.
4. **Act.** Same pot again. No talking at all: charades only.

Pull a phrase, get the table (or your team) to guess it, then grab the next one. Too hard? Skip it: it goes to the back of the queue. Clear every phrase out of the bowl before the buzzer and the turn ends early on the spot: a "cleared the bowl" moment. Still holding a card when the buzzer sounds? The turn ends there and it is your moment.

**Scores (every tier):** 1 point for every phrase your turn gets through. Most points after the Act round wins.

**Drinking:** still holding a card when the buzzer sounds, drink 2.

**Deadly:** still holding a card when the buzzer sounds, take a shot. Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

**Teams:** the host can split the room into teams in the lobby. Scores are kept per player; the team table is everyone's points added up.

All phrases are written by the players, so there is no shared content file: this is a true blank-page game.
