# Truth or Dare

Everyone takes a turn. On yours, pick truth or dare. The prompt is revealed to the whole group at once, out loud, on every screen. There's no secret here, that's the point: the group holds you to it.

1. **Take your turn.** When it's your go, pick Truth or Dare.
2. **Face the prompt.** It shows up on every screen at the same time. You or the host can start it.
3. **Do it, or don't.** You or the host marks it done or skipped.
4. **Rotate.** The host moves on to the next player. Everyone goes round for the number of laps the host set, then the game ends.

**Spice levels:**
- **Mild**: safe for any group, family-friendly party fun.
- **Spicy**: a bit more revealing and cheeky, still comfortable among friends.
- **Wild**: the boldest tier, but still nothing illegal, dangerous or non-consensual, and nobody has to leave the room.

**Scores (every tier):** doing it scores a point, skipping scores nothing. Most points wins.

**Drinking:** do it and walk away clean, skip it and drink 2.

**Deadly:** your first skip of the game is still 2 sips, but skip a second time (or any time after) and that one's a shot. Once your free skip is used, the prompt screen warns you before the next one. Between turns a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall, a Social or a water round.

All prompts are original, written for this game.
