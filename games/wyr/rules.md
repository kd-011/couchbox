# Would You Rather

Two options, A and B. Everyone picks a side. Then the room finds out who ran
with the crowd and who's the odd one out.

1. **Read both options.** They're meant to be a genuinely hard call.
2. **Pick a side.** On own phones, pick A or B in private: nobody can see
   your pick, not even the TV, until the round is revealed. On one phone,
   the host taps each player's name under A or B as the group calls it out
   loud; tap again to move them or to take them off a side.
3. **Reveal.** The split shows as counts and percentages, with who's on
   each side.
4. **Next round.** The host moves on to a new, unused pair. After the set
   number of rounds, the game ends.

**Scores (every tier):** the majority (the bigger side) scores a point each
round for running with the crowd. A dead-even split scores nobody.

**Drinking:** whichever side has fewer votes drinks 2. A dead-even split
means nobody drinks, and a unanimous round means there's no minority either.

**Deadly:** as Drinking, but if you're the only one on the small side (with
3 or more picking), you're the lone wolf: take a shot instead of the sips.
Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a
Waterfall, a Social or a water round.

**Teams:** the host can split the room into teams in the lobby. Everyone
still picks for themselves, but in Deadly, if a whole team (not just one
person) lands on the small side, the team shares one shot instead of each
sipping alone.

**The crowd:** join a full room and you watch from the audience instead. Pick
A or B too, just for yourself: side with the eventual minority and you drink
a sip, but it never changes the real split or anyone's score.

All 170-plus would-you-rather pairs are original, written for this game.
