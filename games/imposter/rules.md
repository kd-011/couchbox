# Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word except the imposter, who only gets the category.

1. **Check your card.** Read your word in private. If it says IMPOSTER, you don't know the word: blend in.
2. **Go round the circle.** Starting with the player shown, each person says one word or short clue about the secret word. Too obvious and the imposter learns it; too vague and you look guilty.
3. **Discuss.** Argue it out before the timer runs out.
4. **Vote.** Everyone votes for who they think the imposter is. On one phone, vote out loud and the host taps who got voted out. A tie means nobody is out.
5. **Reveal.** If the group voted out an imposter, the crew wins the round. Otherwise the imposters win.

**Scores (every tier):** crew win, every crew member scores 1. Imposters win, each imposter scores 2. The game runs for the number of rounds you set (5 by default).

**Drinking:** a caught imposter drinks 3. If the imposters get away with it, the crew all drink 2. Voting for an innocent player costs you 1 more sip, and anyone wrongly voted out hands out 2.

**Deadly:** the imposter who gets voted out takes a shot (their partner still drinks 3). Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.
