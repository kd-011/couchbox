# Never Have I Ever

A classic confession game. Read the prompt, then say whether you have or haven't done it.

1. **Read the prompt.** "Never have I ever ___" appears for everyone.
2. **Answer.** On your own phones, tap "I have" or "I haven't" in private: nobody sees anyone else's answer until the reveal. On one phone, call it out loud and whoever's holding the phone taps each name that's done it.
3. **Reveal.** See the split: who has, who hasn't.
4. **Next round.** The host moves on to a new prompt. After the set number of rounds, the game ends with a final tally of who confessed the most.

**Scores (every tier):** everyone who says "I have" scores a confession point. Most confessions by the end wins (or loses, depending how you look at it).

**Drinking:** everyone who says "I have" drinks 1. At the end, whoever confessed the most drinks 3 for the road.

**Deadly:** as Drinking, but if you're the only one who has (with 3 or more playing), that's a lonely confession: take a shot instead of the sip. Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall, a Social or a water round.

A "Spicy prompts" option mixes in a smaller pool of edgier prompts alongside the main set. All prompts are original, written for this game.
