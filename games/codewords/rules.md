# Codewords

Two teams, one shared grid of words. Hidden among them: your team's words,
the other team's words, some neutral words, and one Bomb.

Each team has a **Caller** who can see the key: which word belongs to which
team, and where the Bomb is. Everyone else on the team is a **Spotter**, and
never sees the key, only what's actually been revealed.

On your turn, your Caller gives a one-word clue and a number, like
"**Ocean, 2**", meaning two of the words on the board relate to that clue.
Your team then taps the board, trying to find your own words:

- Your word: it stays revealed, and you can keep guessing.
- A neutral word, or the other team's word: your turn ends immediately.
- The Bomb: your team instantly loses the game.

Your team may guess up to one more than the number given (so "2" buys you up
to 3 taps), and can stop early any time to keep what you've already banked. A
clue given as "0" means unlimited guesses this turn.

Find every one of your team's words before the other team finds theirs, or
win the moment the other team reveals the Bomb.

The clue can't be a word on the board, or that word with a plural or ending
(seal, seals, sealed). A short board word hiding inside a longer, unrelated
one doesn't count (den is fine inside garden).

**Best with 4 or more.** With 3 players one side is a lone Caller who taps
their own guesses. They can see the whole key, so their clue number is held
at 1. **If a Caller goes quiet** (locked phone, wandered off), the host can
tap Skip turn and the other team carries on.

**One phone:** the active team's Caller gets the phone privately to see the
key and write the clue, then hands it back to the table for everyone to tap
together.

**Drinking:** a wrong guess costs your whole team a sip; hitting the Bomb
costs everyone on that team a bit more.

**Deadly:** whoever hits the Bomb, their whole team takes a shot together.

All content in this game (the word bank, clues and rules) is original.
