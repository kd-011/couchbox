# Most Likely To

A prompt is read out, like "Most likely to become famous" or "Most likely to sleep through an alarm on their wedding day." Everyone decides who in the group fits it best. You can never vote for yourself.

1. **Read the prompt.** It's on the shared screen (or each phone, in a room).
2. **Vote.** On your own phones, pick who fits it best; your pick stays secret until everyone's in, and you can change your mind until then. Once half the table has voted, the rest get 30 seconds, then the votes cast decide it (a locked phone never holds up the room). On one phone, the group calls it out loud and the host taps the winner.
3. **Reveal.** The most-voted player (or players, on a tie) gets crowned for the round. In a room, you'll also see who voted for whom.
4. **Next prompt.** The host moves on. After the set number of rounds, the final crown count decides the game.

**Scores (every tier):** the crowned player scores a point each round (everyone tied for the crown scores). Most crowns at the end wins.

**Drinking:** the crowned player drinks 2. A clean tie means everyone tied drinks 1 instead, nobody escapes a true tie.

**Deadly:** crowned by every single vote takes a shot instead of the 2 sips. On own phones that means 3 or more votes cast and every vote from everyone else went to you. On one phone the group calls the crown out loud, so a crown the host taps counts as unanimous. Either way it is a shot only when no Deadly moment landed in the last 3 minutes (otherwise it's the usual 2 sips, so a small group can't be shot every round). The screen shows the Deadly note only while a crown would be a shot. Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall, a Social or a water round.

**Teams:** the host can split the room into teams in the lobby. Crowns and
drinks still happen to individuals, except a unanimous crown in Deadly: then
the crowned player's whole team shares the one shot.

**The crowd:** join a full room and you watch from the audience instead.
Vote for who fits the prompt too, just for yourself: pick someone who isn't
crowned and you drink, but it never changes who actually wins the round.

All prompts are original to this game.
