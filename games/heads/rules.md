# Celebrity Heads

One player is the guesser. Everyone else can see a famous name, thing or place. The guesser can't.

1. **Take the hot seat.** On one phone, hold it to your forehead, screen facing out. On your own phones, the guesser just doesn't look at anyone else's screen.
2. **Ask and guess.** The guesser asks yes/no questions ("Am I a person?") or takes a straight guess. The others answer and can give clues, but never say the word.
3. **Got it or pass.** When the guesser gets it, tap Got it. Stuck? Tap Pass for a new one. Keep going until the timer runs out.
4. **Rotate.** Everyone takes a turn in the hot seat. Most correct guesses after the last turn wins.

**Scores (every tier):** one point per correct guess. Most points after the last turn wins.

**Drinking:** every pass costs the guesser a sip. Finish a turn with nothing guessed and drink 3. After the last turn, the lowest score drinks 5 and the top score hands out 5.

**Deadly:** passes are still a sip, but finish a turn with nothing guessed and you take a shot. After the last turn, last place takes a shot (a tie for last spins the wheel) and the top score hands out 5. Between turns a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.
