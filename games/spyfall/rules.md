# Spyfall

Everyone at the table gets the same secret location and a role there, except the Spy, who gets nothing but the knowledge that they're the Spy.

1. **Check your card.** Crew: your location and your role there. The Spy: just "you are the Spy".
2. **Talk in character.** Go round asking each other in-character questions about the place without ever saying its name. Crew want questions precise enough to prove they belong, but not so precise they hand the Spy the answer. The Spy bluffs along and listens for clues.
3. **Call a vote any time.** Anyone can get the table voting on who they think the Spy is. On one phone, vote out loud and the host taps who got voted out. A tie means nobody is out and discussion carries on.
4. **The Spy can also guess.** In a room (everyone on their own phone), the Spy can try to name the location outright at any point instead of waiting to be voted on. Guess right and they win on the spot; guess wrong and they're revealed immediately. On one phone, the Spy can only resolve this by being voted out first (see below): there's no private way to let them guess mid-discussion without a second phone.
5. **Caught? Last chance.** If the table votes out the Spy, they get one short last chance to name the location anyway. Guess it and the Spy wins regardless of being caught; run out of time (or guess wrong) and the crew wins.
6. **Wrong vote? The Spy gets away.** Vote out someone who isn't the Spy and the round ends there and the Spy escapes clean.

**Scores (every tier):** the Spy wins (by any route) scores 2. The crew wins (the Spy caught and stumped, or a wrong voluntary guess), every crew member scores 1. The game runs for the number of rounds you set (6 by default).

**Drinking:** the crew all drink 2 when the Spy wins; when the crew wins, the Spy drinks 3. Voting for an innocent player costs you 1 more sip, and anyone wrongly voted out hands out 2.

**Deadly:** a caught, stumped Spy takes a shot. Between rounds a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

All locations and roles are original to this game.
