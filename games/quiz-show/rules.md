# Quiz Show

A full quiz night with a board, a buzzer and a final wager.

1. **Pick a tile.** The host picks a tile off the board. Each tile is a category and a round type: multiple choice, a buzzer round, an emoji round or true-or-false.
2. **Play the round.**
   - *Multiple choice* and *true-or-false*: answer against the clock. Faster correct answers score more.
   - *Emoji round*: guess what the emoji clue means, from four options.
   - *Buzzer round*: first to buzz gets to answer. Get it wrong and the buzzer reopens for everyone else. On your own phone, type your answer and it is judged automatically. On one phone, the quizmaster holding it hears the answer out loud and taps Correct or Wrong.
3. **Reveal, then standings.** The correct answer is revealed, points are added, and the leaderboard updates before the next tile.
4. **Final wager.** Once the board is empty, everyone wagers any amount up to their score, then answers one last question. Get it right and you gain the wager; get it wrong and you lose it.

**Pass and play:** everyone is split into two teams. Whoever is holding the phone reads the questions aloud and taps on behalf of each team, like a quizmaster running the show.

**Own phones:** everyone answers from their own screen; a TV or laptop can show the shared board, buzzer and leaderboard. The host can split the room into teams in the lobby: tap an answer or buzz for your team, any member can do it, and the whole team shares the score and the drinks.

**The crowd:** join a full room and you watch from the audience instead. Bet on who scores each round from your phone: get it wrong and you drink, get it right and nothing happens.

**Scores (every tier):** points for speed and accuracy. The biggest score after the final wager wins.

**Drinking:** a wrong answer is a sip. Whoever is last on the leaderboard after each round drinks a sip. Losing the final wager costs 2. On one phone, a team's drinks go to every member of the team.

**Deadly:** the Drinking sips, plus Double or Nothing: three right answers in a row put you on it for your next answer. Get that one right and it scores double; get it wrong and you take a shot (the whole team, on one phone). After the final wager, last place takes a shot. Between tiles a Deadly card may turn up: Shot Roulette, Tall Poppy, a Waterfall or a water round.

Most multiple-choice and true-or-false questions are from the [Open Trivia Database](https://opentdb.com) (opentdb.com), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The emoji round is original.
